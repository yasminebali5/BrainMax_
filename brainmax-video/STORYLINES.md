# BrainMax walkthrough — storyline options

Status: option 1 selected; storyboard review pending. These are three different
films, not three title treatments. No composition has been built yet.

## Shared source facts

- The demo uses a synthetic learner, Nora, on an isolated local server and
  database. The captured interface is the real BrainMax app.
- Nora's six-question machine-learning baseline is 83/100: Evaluation 50%,
  Generalization 100%, Foundations 100%, and Learning systems 100%. The result
  also shows 95/100 confidence calibration, 100% independent answers, and an
  11.5-second average response time.
- Nora passed the first learning-path activity, Sequence memory, at 100%.
  The ten-activity path is still in progress at 1/10.
- Brain Twin shows one completed session and marks its 58–100 projection range
  as very low confidence. Do not present these short practice scores as a
  validated cognitive or ability measure.
- The AI Coach page shows the real score and suggested focus. A live chat reply
  has not been requested; the configured provider may receive the message and
  learning context if we send one.

## 1. Recommended — The score finds its next step

- **Device:** Result first; one real score becomes a breadcrumb through the
  learner's next steps.
- **World:** The actual BrainMax workspace, opening on the assessment result
  and then rewinding to Nora's account setup before moving forward again.
- **Form:** Result-first reveal followed by an object-to-object chain: the
  score's Evaluation label carries from assessment to practice to Brain Twin.
- **What the viewer sees and feels:** The 83/100 result appears first, but the
  story asks what Nora can do next. We return to signup, follow her goal into
  the baseline, and let the 50% Evaluation result lead to a first passed
  practice step. The close frames the score as a starting point, not a verdict.
- **First two seconds:** The real `83/100` score lands full-frame; the caption
  reads, “What comes after the score?”
- **Beats:** Result hook → account creation → privacy and voice choices →
  learning goal → six-question baseline → all assessment results → first
  learning-path pass → AI Coach and Brain/Pinky support → Brain Twin's
  one-session snapshot → “One small step at a time.”
- **Look:** App-derived ice `#f0f3fa`, ink `#172b46`, navy `#395886`, blue
  `#638ecb`, and teal `#148b80`; Manrope display with DM Sans body. Real
  screens stay recognizable while the relevant result is enlarged in place.
- **Sound:** Offline Kokoro narration; no soundtrack or added media, keeping
  the real interface and spoken guidance in focus.

## 2. Nora's first learning session

- **Device:** One learner's real goal travels through the whole first session.
- **World:** The actual app, seen as Nora moves from signup into her first
  machine-learning practice.
- **Form:** A forward, cursor-led object-to-object chain; each screen's real
  action opens the next.
- **What the viewer sees and feels:** The viewer starts where Nora starts:
  creating an account, choosing her preferences, and describing a goal. The
  assessment turns that goal into a baseline; the learning path and Brain Twin
  show how that first session is carried forward.
- **First two seconds:** The real profile field “What would you like to learn?”
  is selected; the caption reads, “Start with what you want to learn.”
- **Beats:** Signup → settings → profile and goal → assessment questions →
  83/100 result and section breakdown → first path activity and 1/10 progress
  → Brain and Pinky voice controls → AI Coach insights → Brain Twin history →
  return to Nora's next step.
- **Look:** The same BrainMax palette and typography, with the goal field as
  the visual carrier; close crops return to the full workspace before each
  transition.
- **Sound:** Offline Kokoro narration, paced like a calm guided session; no
  soundtrack or added media.

## 3. Bold — Two coaches, one learning trail

- **Device:** Brain plans while Pinky encourages; both meet at the same
  learner goal and evidence.
- **World:** The real Brain and Pinky companion interface, anchored to Nora's
  assessment and Brain Twin screens.
- **Form:** A split-screen-to-one duet: the two companion states alternate,
  then resolve into the single learning path they support.
- **What the viewer sees and feels:** The film opens on the two real companion
  choices, then traces the shared context behind them: Nora's goal, her
  83/100 baseline, the Evaluation focus, and one passed activity. Brain Twin
  brings the two voices back to one remembered learning history.
- **First two seconds:** The real Brain and Pinky controls enter together;
  the caption reads, “A plan, and a little encouragement.”
- **Beats:** Brain/Pinky hook → signup and preferences → Nora's goal →
  assessment → score and suggested focus → Brain's plan → Pinky's support →
  first path result → Brain Twin's cautious one-session view → both voices
  resolve to the next step.
- **Look:** App-derived blue/navy for Brain and the existing violet/teal
  accents for Pinky; use the actual companion artwork and interface, never
  invented robot screens.
- **Sound:** Offline Kokoro narration with distinct Brain/Pinky reads for any
  approved demo exchange; no soundtrack or added media.

## Assumptions

Approximately 4:30, 16:9 at 1920×1080, English captions, and offline Kokoro
narration are already in the brief. The app captures use only the isolated
synthetic demo account. No paid media is planned; no background-music track is
available in the project or local reuse cache. No live OpenRouter chat request
has been sent.
